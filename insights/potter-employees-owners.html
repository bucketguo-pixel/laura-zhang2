Company Insight · May 2024

Potter Employees Become Owners

Insight Focus

DateMay 2024
CompanyPotter Global Technologies
ThemeBroad-Based Ownership

Executive Summary

Potter Global Technologies is a St. Louis-based manufacturer of fire and life-safety equipment. After KKR acquired the company in November 2023, Potter implemented a broad-based employee ownership program in March 2024.

The program enabled approximately 850 employees to become owners. It created a more direct link between daily operating contribution and the company’s long-term objectives, strengthening alignment across the organization.

Ownership in Practice

Following the ownership program, the organization was able to reinforce a shared operating mindset: employees were not positioned only as participants in execution, but as contributors to enterprise value. This helped strengthen responsibility, engagement and confidence in future growth.

Strategic Takeaways

  • Employee ownership can improve organizational belonging and alignment with business goals.
  • Post-investment management is not limited to financial and operating optimization; it also includes incentive design.
  • Broad participation creates a more direct connection between enterprise growth and employee contribution.
  • Ownership culture can support long-term performance and organizational stability.
