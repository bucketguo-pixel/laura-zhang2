Professional Overview

Juan de Ochoa joined KKR Capstone in 2011 and serves as Co-Head of KKR Capstone EMEA. He is also a member of KKR’s EMEA Portfolio Management Committee.

At KKR Capstone, he leads and supports value-creation initiatives across the EMEA portfolio, with an emphasis on operating efficiency, performance improvement, EBITDA expansion, procurement effectiveness and supply-chain transformation.

Previous Experience

Prior to joining KKR Capstone, Juan was a Director at AlixPartners, where he focused on procurement and supply-chain work. Earlier in his career, he served as general manager and principal shareholder of an automotive Tier 2 supplier with plants in Spain and Slovakia.

Education & Strengths

He holds a degree in economics and business administration from Université Catholique de Louvain in Belgium and has completed specialized training in operations management.
